**PanTS (Pancreatic Tumor Segmentation)** is an AI-driven research project focused on the **segmentation of pancreatic tumours from medical images**, with the goal of supporting accurate and reproducible computational analysis.

## Objective

Develop and evaluate deep learning methods for:

- Pancreatic tumour detection and segmentation
- Medical image preprocessing and analysis
- AI-based segmentation modelling
- Quantitative evaluation of segmentation performance

## Research Workflow

**Medical Images → Preprocessing → Tumour Segmentation → Model Evaluation → Clinical Analysis**

## Research Focus

The project explores modern **deep learning and computer vision** approaches for pancreatic tumour segmentation, with emphasis on accuracy, robustness, and reproducibility.
